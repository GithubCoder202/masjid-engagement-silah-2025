Community Rules & Guidelines

Our commitment to a safe, respectful, and Islamic-centered space for everyone.

1

🤲 Islamic Etiquette (Adab)

All discussions must be conducted with kindness, respect, and good character. Avoid harsh language, backbiting, and slander.

"Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, let him speak good or remain silent." — Bukhari
2

🕌 Stay on Topic

Keep discussions relevant to Islam, community, mentorship, and spiritual growth. Off-topic posts may be removed.

"Part of a person's good Islam is leaving what does not concern him." — Tirmidhi
3

🚫 No Hate Speech

Racism, sectarianism, misogyny, and any form of hate speech are strictly prohibited. We are one Ummah.

"No one is a believer until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself." — Bukhari & Muslim
4

📖 Cite Sources

When sharing Islamic knowledge, cite Quranic verses and authentic Hadith. Avoid spreading misinformation.

"Whoever speaks about the Quran without knowledge, let him take his seat in the Fire." — Tirmidhi
5

🤝 Respect Privacy

Do not share personal information of others without consent. Protect your own privacy as well.

"The Muslim is the one from whose tongue and hand the Muslims are safe." — Bukhari
6

🧠 AI Moderation

All posts and comments are reviewed by AI moderation. Inappropriate content will be flagged or removed.

"Enjoin good and forbid evil." — Quran 3:104
7

⭐ Report Responsibly

Use the report feature only for genuine violations. False reporting may lead to restrictions.

"Allah loves moderation in all affairs." — Bukhari
8

🕋 Be Supportive

Encourage one another in faith. Celebrate milestones and offer support during hardships.

"The believers are like a single body." — Bukhari

The Blessings of Mosque Community

Discover why the masjid is the heart of Muslim life and how being part of a mosque community strengthens your faith, builds brotherhood/sisterhood, and brings you closer to Allah.

Why Join a Masjid?

The mosque is more than a place of prayer — it is the center of the Muslim community. The Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) said:

"Whoever goes to the mosque in the morning and evening, Allah will prepare for him a place in Paradise for every morning and evening." — Bukhari & Muslim

Being part of a mosque community brings countless benefits:

  • Spiritual Growth: Regular attendance at the mosque helps maintain your connection to Allah through congregational prayers, Quran recitation, and Islamic classes.
  • Brotherhood/Sisterhood: The mosque is a place to meet fellow Muslims, build lasting friendships, and support one another in times of need.
  • Learning Opportunities: Mosques offer classes, halaqas, and lectures that deepen your understanding of Islam.
  • Community Service: Engage in charitable activities, food drives, and other initiatives that benefit the local community.
  • Support for New Muslims: Converts find a welcoming environment to learn and integrate into the Muslim community.
  • Family Involvement: Mosques provide activities for children and youth, fostering a strong Islamic identity from a young age.

By joining a mosque community, you not only earn immense rewards but also contribute to the unity and strength of the Ummah. Take the step today — your local masjid is waiting for you!

    🕌 Five Pillars of Islam

    The foundation of a Muslim's faith and practice.

    ☝️

    1. Shahada الشهادة

    The declaration of faith: "Lā ilāha illā Allāh, Muḥammadun rasūlu Allāh" — "There is no god but Allah, and Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah." This is the core belief that distinguishes a Muslim. It affirms the oneness of God and the prophethood of Muhammad (peace be upon him).

    "Whoever says 'Lā ilāha illā Allāh' sincerely from his heart will enter Paradise." — Bukhari
    🧎

    2. Salah الصلاة

    Performing the five daily prayers (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) at prescribed times. Prayer is a direct connection between the believer and Allah, fostering discipline, humility, and spiritual awareness. It includes recitation of Quranic verses, bowing, and prostration.

    "The first thing a person will be judged for on the Day of Judgment is prayer." — An-Nasa'i
    💰

    3. Zakat الزكاة

    Obligatory charity given annually on surplus wealth above a minimum threshold (nisab). Zakat purifies one's wealth and helps the poor, the needy, and those in debt. It is a means of social solidarity and economic justice.

    "Zakat is not a tax; it is a right of the poor in the wealth of the rich."
    🌙

    4. Sawm الصوم

    Fasting from dawn to sunset during the month of Ramadan. Muslims abstain from food, drink, and other physical needs during the day. Fasting teaches self-restraint, empathy for the hungry, and spiritual reflection. It is a time for increased prayer and Quran recitation.

    "Fasting is a shield; when one of you is fasting, let him not behave foolishly." — Bukhari & Muslim
    🕋

    5. Hajj الحج

    Pilgrimage to the holy city of Mecca in Saudi Arabia, required once in a lifetime for those who are physically and financially able. Hajj takes place during the Islamic month of Dhul-Hijjah and involves a series of rituals that commemorate the actions of Prophet Ibrahim and his family.

    "Whoever performs Hajj for the sake of Allah and avoids obscenity and wrongdoing will return sinless as the day his mother gave birth to him." — Bukhari
